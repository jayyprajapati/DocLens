# AeroPro X2 Drone — Owner's Manual

| Operating temperature | -10 °C to 40 °C |

## Charging

A full charge takes about 75 minutes with the supplied 65 W charger. Do not charge the battery below 0 °C. The battery indicator shows four LEDs; a single blinking LED means the charge is below 25%.

## Flight safety

Do not fly in rain, snow, or winds above 38 km/h. In most countries drones over 250 g must be registered with the national aviation authority. Keep the drone within line of sight at all times. Do not fly within 5 km of an airport.

## Return-to-home

The drone returns home automatically when the battery falls to 20%, when the controller signal is lost for more than 6 seconds, or when the Return button is held for 2 seconds. The default return altitude is 30 m and can be changed in the app between 20 m and 120 m.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Gimbal error on startup | Gimbal clamp left on | Remove the clamp and restart |
| Compass interference warning | Metal objects nearby | Move at least 5 m away and recalibrate |
| Short flight time | Cold battery | Warm the battery to above 15 °C before flight |
| No video feed | Controller out of range or firmware outdated | Move closer and update firmware |

## Warranty

The AeroPro X2 has a 12-month limited warranty. Crash damage and water damage are not covered. The battery is covered for 6 months or 200 charge cycles, whichever comes first.
