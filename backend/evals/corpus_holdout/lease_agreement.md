# Commercial Lease Agreement — Harbor View Offices

This Lease is made on 1 February 2025 between Harbor View Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Kestrel Design Studio Inc. ("Tenant") for Suite 410 at 88 Wharf Street.

## 1. Term

The initial term is 36 months, beginning on 1 March 2025 and ending on 28 February 2028. The Tenant may renew for one further term of 24 months by giving written notice at least 120 days before the end of the initial term.

## 2. Rent

Base rent is $8,450 per month for the first 12 months. Base rent increases by 3.5% at the start of each subsequent lease year. Rent is due on the first day of each month. A late fee of 5% of the overdue amount applies if rent is more than 7 days late.

## 3. Security deposit

The Tenant paid a security deposit of $16,900, equal to two months of initial base rent. The deposit is returned within 30 days after the lease ends, less any deductions for damage beyond normal wear and tear.

## 4. Permitted use and hours

The premises may be used only as a design studio and general office. Building access outside 7:00 to 19:00 on weekdays requires a key card; after-hours HVAC is billed at $45 per hour. Overnight occupancy is prohibited.

## 5. Parking

The Tenant is allocated 3 reserved parking spaces in Garage B at no extra charge. Additional spaces may be rented at $180 per space per month, subject to availability.

## 6. Maintenance and repairs

The Landlord maintains the building structure, roof, elevators, and common areas. The Tenant is responsible for repairs inside the suite, including interior walls, floors, and fixtures. The Tenant must notify the Landlord of any damage within 48 hours.

## 7. Subletting and assignment

The Tenant may not sublet or assign the lease without the Landlord's prior written consent, which may not be unreasonably withheld. The Landlord has 15 business days to respond to a request.

## 8. Early termination

The Tenant may terminate early after month 24 by giving 90 days' written notice and paying a termination fee equal to 4 months of then-current base rent. The Landlord may not terminate early except for uncured default.

## 9. Insurance

The Tenant must carry commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence. The Landlord must be named as an additional insured.

## 10. Pets

Pets are not permitted in the premises, except service animals.
