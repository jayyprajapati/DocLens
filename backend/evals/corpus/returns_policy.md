# Orion Hardware — Returns and Warranty Policy

Orion Hardware is the consumer devices brand of Vantora Systems. This document describes the current returns and warranty policy, version 2, effective March 1, 2026. Version 2 replaces version 1, under which the return window was 30 days.

## Return window

Customers may return any unopened or unused Orion product within 45 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened products may be returned within 45 days but are subject to a 15% restocking fee, except when the product is defective. Products returned after 45 days are not eligible for a refund but may qualify for warranty service.

## Non-returnable items

The following items cannot be returned once opened: software license keys, custom-engraved products, and Orion Care subscription cards. Gift cards are never refundable.

## Return shipping

Return shipping is free for defective products and for orders delivered to the wrong address. For all other returns the customer pays return shipping, which is deducted from the refund at a flat rate of $8.50 within the continental United States.

## Warranty

All Orion devices carry a 2-year limited warranty from the date of purchase. The warranty covers manufacturing defects. It does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, or damage from unauthorized repairs. The Orion Pulse smartwatch has a shorter 1-year warranty on its battery only.

Customers who buy Orion Care receive accidental damage coverage for up to 3 incidents over the life of the plan. Orion Care costs $79 per year for phones and $49 per year for smartwatches.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days of the returned product being received at the Orion warehouse in Reno, Nevada. Refunds for orders paid with store credit are issued as store credit.

## Contacting support

Customers can start a return at the Orion Support Portal or by calling support. Phone support is available Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 20:00 Pacific Time. There is no weekend phone support; weekend requests are handled by chat only.
