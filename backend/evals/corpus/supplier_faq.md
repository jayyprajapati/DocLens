# Supplier FAQ

This page answers common questions from Vantora Systems suppliers.

## Payment terms

Standard payment terms for suppliers are net 45 days from invoice receipt. An early-payment discount of 2% applies when an invoice is paid within 10 days.

## Onboarding

New suppliers must complete the vendor questionnaire within 14 days of approval. Suppliers handling payment data must also provide a SOC 2 report.

## Disputes

Invoice disputes must be raised within 30 days of the invoice date. Disputes are reviewed by the Finance team, who respond within 5 business days.
