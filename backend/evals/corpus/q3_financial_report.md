# Vantora Systems — Q3 2026 Financial Report

## Summary

Vantora Systems closed Q3 2026 with total revenue of $48.2 million, a 14% increase over Q3 2025 ($42.3 million). Gross margin was 71.5%. Operating income was $6.9 million, compared with $4.1 million in the prior-year quarter. The company ended the quarter with $112 million in cash and no outstanding debt.

## Revenue by segment

| Segment | Q3 2026 revenue | Year-over-year growth |
|---|---|---|
| Aurora Payments | $27.6 million | 19% |
| Orion Hardware | $15.1 million | 6% |
| Professional Services | $5.5 million | 2% |

Aurora Payments was the fastest-growing segment and now accounts for 57% of total revenue. Orion Hardware growth was held back by supply constraints on the Orion Pulse smartwatch, which was out of stock for three weeks in August.

## Headcount

Vantora ended the quarter with 612 employees, up from 571 at the end of Q2. Engineering accounts for 287 of those employees. The company opened a new office in Toronto in September with 18 initial hires.

## Customer metrics

Aurora processed $9.4 billion in total payment volume during the quarter. The number of active Aurora merchants reached 31,400. Net revenue retention for Aurora was 118%. Orion Hardware shipped 212,000 devices, of which the Orion Pulse accounted for 74,000.

## Outlook

Management expects Q4 2026 revenue between $51 million and $53 million. Full-year 2026 revenue is guided to $190 million to $194 million. Capital expenditures for the full year are expected to be approximately $11 million, primarily for the new Toronto office and data center expansion. The company does not plan to pay a dividend in 2026.

## Risks

Key risks include dependence on a single contract manufacturer in Shenzhen for Orion devices, regulatory changes to payment processing in the European Union, and foreign exchange exposure, with roughly 22% of revenue denominated in euros and pounds.
