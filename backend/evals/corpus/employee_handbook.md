# Vantora Systems Employee Handbook (2026 Edition)

Welcome to Vantora Systems. This handbook describes the policies that apply to all full-time employees.

## Working hours and location

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in each employee's local time zone. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Employees based in the Austin office must be on site on Tuesdays and Wednesdays for team days. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from a Director or above.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off per calendar year. PTO accrues monthly. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year; any additional unused days are forfeited on January 31. Employees with more than 5 years of service receive 30 days instead of 25.

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 10 sick days per year, which do not carry over. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave may be taken within the first 12 months after birth or adoption. Leave must be taken in blocks of at least 2 weeks.

## Learning and development budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of $1,800. This covers courses, books, and conferences. Conference travel is approved separately and is not deducted from the learning budget. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Equipment

New hires receive a laptop of their choice: a 14-inch MacBook Pro or a Dell XPS 15. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. A one-time home office stipend of $600 is paid within the first 30 days of employment.

## Expense reimbursement

Expenses under $75 do not require a receipt. Expenses of $75 or more require an itemized receipt. All expense reports must be submitted within 45 days of the expense date through the Ledgerly system. Reimbursement is paid on the next bi-weekly payroll cycle after approval.

## On-call compensation

Engineers on the on-call rotation receive a stipend of $350 per week of on-call duty. If an engineer is paged outside business hours and works more than 2 hours on an incident, they receive a compensatory half day off.

## Security requirements

All employees must use a hardware security key for single sign-on. Passwords for internal systems must be at least 14 characters. Employees must complete security awareness training every 12 months. Personal USB storage devices are prohibited on company laptops.

## Performance reviews

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December. Promotion cycles run only after the December review. Salary adjustments take effect on March 1.
